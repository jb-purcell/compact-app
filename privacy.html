Compact

Privacy policy. Last updated November 18, 2026.

Compact collects nothing. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

The camera

Compact uses your iPhone's front camera for one thing: to show you a live mirror on your own screen. The picture is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. When you hold the picture still, it stays on screen only until you let it go or leave the app.

The one-time purchase

Compact is free to use. If you buy Compact Complete, the purchase is made with Apple, through your Apple Account, the same way as any other App Store purchase. We never see your name, your email, your card or your Apple Account. Apple tells the app on your iPhone that the purchase is yours, and that is all the app learns.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your light, warmth, zoom, mirror direction and finish, so Compact opens the way you left it.
  • Whether Compact Complete is yours, so the app opens unlocked.
  • A count of how many times you have opened the mirror, used only to decide when to ask once for an App Store rating.

These never leave your iPhone. Deleting Compact deletes them. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account, so you can restore it at any time at no cost.

What we receive

Nothing from the app. Apple gives us sales totals, and anonymous crash totals if you have agreed to share them with developers in your iPhone's settings. Neither says who you are.

Children

Compact collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
