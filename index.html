Compact

A pocket mirror with good light, for iPhone. Help and contact.

Write to rjprovisionstx@gmail.com and a person will answer. The answers below cover most questions.

The mirror is dark

Compact needs the camera to show you the mirror. Open Settings, then Apps, then Compact, and turn on Camera. The light works either way.

What is free, and what is Compact Complete?

The mirror, the light and every iPhone Duo layout are free. Compact Complete is one purchase, made once, that adds warm to cool light, zoom, hold still, the flip and four more finishes. It is not a subscription and it never renews.

I bought Compact Complete and it is locked again

  1. Tap any control with a small lock.
  2. Tap Restore Purchase.

Restoring is free. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. On a new iPhone it usually comes back by itself within a moment of opening Compact.

Does my family get it too?

Yes. Compact Complete is shared with your Family Sharing group at no extra cost.

I have a code

Tap any control with a small lock, then Have a code? and enter it. You can also redeem it in the App Store: tap your picture, then Redeem Gift Card or Code.

I would like a refund

Apple handles every App Store payment and refund. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose Compact.

Does Compact record me?

No. The camera only feeds the mirror on your screen. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. See the privacy policy.
